Privacy Policy

Personal Finance Manager

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how Personal Finance Manager ("the App") handles your information. The App is designed to work entirely on your device — please read below for full details.

Data We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on any remote server. All information you enter — expenses, income, debts, budgets, accounts, saving goals, and optional profile details (name, email, photo) — is created and stored by you, for you, entirely on your device.

Local Data Storage

Your financial data is stored in a local database on your device and is never automatically synced to any cloud service. The Backup & Restore feature lets you export a copy of your data to a file of your choosing — this only happens when you actively choose to back up or restore, and the resulting file stays under your control.

Financial Information Handling

Amounts, categories, account names, and notes you enter are used solely to power the App's own features (dashboard, statistics, budgets). This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and we have no access to it.

Advertisements

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements on select screens. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising from your device's settings. Ads are never shown on screens where you enter or manage sensitive information, such as Add Expense, Add Income, Profile, Security, Settings, or Accounts.

Permissions We Use

  • SMS (optional): to automatically detect bank transaction messages and suggest expenses. You can deny or revoke this at any time — the App remains fully usable without it.
  • Notifications: for reminders and budget alerts you choose to enable.
  • Biometric/Fingerprint: to unlock the App using your device's screen lock, if you enable App Lock.

SMS Processing

If you grant SMS permission, the App scans messages on your device to identify ones that look like bank transaction alerts, and extracts the amount, merchant, and date to suggest as an expense. This processing happens entirely on your device — message content is never uploaded or sent anywhere. You review and approve every suggested transaction before it's added.

Analytics & Third-Party Services

Other than Google AdMob (see Advertisements above), the App does not use analytics, crash-reporting, or any other third-party tracking service.

Your Rights

You can delete all data stored by the App at any time from Settings → Delete all data. Because everything is stored locally, uninstalling the App also removes all its data from your device. You can review and change any permission at any time from your device's system settings.
